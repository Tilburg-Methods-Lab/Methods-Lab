# Methods-Lab

Methods Lab is a PhD-led initiative that creates a collaborative and informal space for researchers to explore, discuss, and apply research methods together.

The Lab brings together PhD researchers and colleagues who are interested in expanding their methodological knowledge, exchanging experiences, and learning from one another. Rather than focusing only on methodological theory, the Methods Lab combines discussion with practical application and peer learning.

Sessions are held every two months and typically last 90 minutes. Depending on the topic, sessions can take different formats:

Method Journal Club — discussing a selected research paper with a particular focus on its methodological approach.

Expert Talk — inviting experienced researchers, senior scholars, or PhD researchers to introduce a method and share their practical experience.

Hands-on Workshop  — working directly with methods, data, and code, including reproducing or adapting existing analyses.

Some sessions may combine these formats. Materials, code, datasets (where sharing is permitted), and other resources for the sessions are made available through this repository.

The aim of the Methods Lab is to create a low-threshold environment where researchers can learn new methods, ask questions, experiment with tools and approaches, and share methodological knowledge across projects and disciplines.
